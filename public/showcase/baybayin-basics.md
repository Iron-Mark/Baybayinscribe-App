# Sample: Baybayin vowels and practice

This is a small bundled showcase, not a recovered lesson from the original database.
You can explore the app without an account or a working content server.
Sample completion and quiz scores are not saved to an account.

## Start with the vowels

- **ᜀ** — A
- **ᜁ** — E / I
- **ᜂ** — O / U

Baybayin is a syllabic writing system. Consonant characters normally include an
inherent **a** vowel; marks called **kudlit** change that vowel.
